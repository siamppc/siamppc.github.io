FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Google Ads Questions, Answered Straight.

Clear, honest answers to common questions about Google Ads and how we would work together.

How does it work, and how do we start?

It starts with a free 30-minute strategy call about your business, goals and current campaigns. Then I research your market, build and launch your campaigns, and keep optimizing while sending you clear reports.

What's included in the free Google Ads audit?

I review where your account may be losing opportunities or wasting budget: campaign structure, keywords, search intent, negative keywords, ad relevance, location targeting, bidding and tracking. You get a clear picture of what's working and what needs fixing.

Do you work with businesses in my industry?

Most likely. I work with service businesses across many industries, including home services, contractors, law firms, healthcare, automotive, real estate and beauty. If yours isn't on that list, message me and we'll talk it through.

Do I need a large advertising budget to get started?

Not necessarily. The right budget depends on your industry, location, competition and customer value. I build the strategy around the budget you have, focusing on high-intent searches so it works as efficiently as possible.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Management comes in three plans, Starter, Growth and Scale, based on how many campaigns you need and the level of support. Your ad spend is separate from my management fee. On a free call we'll find the plan that fits.

Do you require a long-term contract?

I want you to stay because it's working, not because you feel locked in. Engagement terms are explained clearly before work begins, so you know what to expect from both sides.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. The account stays in your name and I work in it with manager access, so your data and history are always yours.

How quickly will I start seeing results?

Campaigns can start bringing in traffic and inquiries soon after launch, but meaningful results depend on your market, budget, competition, offer and website. I focus first on solid structure and tracking, then use real data to cut waste and improve.

How do you report results?

Every month you get a clear report on calls, leads, cost per lead and ad spend, plus a live dashboard you can check any time.
